## Where this comes from

`id:7c75` -- "`/relay human` section 3(a) mandates a REVIEW_ME apply step its own tooling
cannot perform" -- was CLOSED 2026-09-10 by `relay/scripts/review-box-tick.py` (`ee0b17ed`,
wrapped-title fix `c9a24cf5`). That tool composes the enclosing `## ` section, flips exactly
one checkbox, and hands the section back to `meeting/md-merge.py update-sections` under its
flock. It needs an anchor: either an anchored `<!-- id:XXXX -->` on the checkbox line, or a
`## ` heading that uniquely encloses the box.

The measurement recorded in that commit, over **431 open boxes across 34 repos**:

* **311 boxes** sit under a unique `## ` heading and are now tickable.
* **120 boxes in 13 repos** have no `## ` heading at all and no anchored marker. For those,
  3(a) still has no flock-safe apply path, and the correct behaviour remains what two triage
  agents already did independently: refuse to tick rather than reach for `Edit`.

Named counts from that measurement: it-infra 22, mathematical-writing 15, git-annex 12,
ai-codebench 11, project_manager 11, zkWhale 10, yinyang-puzzle 7, plus the remainder spread
over the other six repos.

`review-box-tick.py` mints neither a heading nor a marker, deliberately -- writing an anchor
into another repo's ledger is not a tick, and the tool's refusals (exit 4 "no `## ` heading
above the box", exit 5 "the heading REPEATS in the file") are the loud form of this gap.

## Why it is `[INPUT - decision]` and not `[ROUTINE]`

The two candidate fixes are not equivalent and the choice is not the executor's:

* **(a) Sweep one `## ` heading per box.** Cheap per repo, but it invents structure in
  thirteen other repos' human-facing evidence files, and a heading that later stops being
  unique silently breaks the anchor again (exit 5 is the guard, not a fix).
* **(b) Mint anchored `<!-- id:XXXX -->` markers onto checkbox lines.** This is the shape
  `update-ids` already wants and it survives re-heading, but most of these boxes carry NO id
  at all (7 of 21 live boxes in this repo alone are id-less), so it means minting tokens for
  evidence that may never become an item -- and a minted token that nothing owns is exactly
  the failure `id:fe67`/`id:02fe`/`id:a192` were promoted for on 2026-09-10.
* **(c) Accept the gap.** Defensible: those 13 repos' boxes are read by a human anyway, and
  the refusal is loud rather than silent.

Whichever is chosen, it is a fleet-wide write into other repos' ledgers and therefore an
owner call, not a review's.
