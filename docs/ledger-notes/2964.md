## From TODO

**`tools/ledger-shrink.py`'s structural keep-set regex `<!--[^>]*-->` over-matches across
PROSE, splicing sentence fragments into head lines it was supposed to protect.** Found
2026-09-07 by the `/relay human --all` tier-(a) sweep; independently re-verified here
against the live `TODO.md` rather than taken on the reporting agent's word.

The catch-all exists for a good reason and `CLAUDE.md` describes it as a strength: it
subsumes `routed:` / `children:` / `xledger-ok:` / `gated-on:` and any marker minted later,
so a new marker type cannot silently fall out of the keep-set. The defect is the same
property seen from the other side. `[^>]*` is not anchored to a real marker shape, so when
an item's own PROSE contains a bare `<!--` inside backticks — which happens whenever an
item is ABOUT the marker syntax — the match runs from that `<!--` forward to the next `>`
anywhere in the body, and everything in between is hoisted onto the head line.

**Two live corrupted head lines, both verified by reading the file:**

- `TODO.md:722` (`id:5817`) ends:
  `` <!-- id:XXXX --> <!-- id:' REVIEW_ME.md` rises as new boxes are surfaced, and closing one needs no `Edit`. ``
  A mid-sentence fragment, swallowed from a backticked `<!--` up to the next `>`.
- `TODO.md:34` (`id:ee62`) ends:
  `` <!-- id:XXXX --> <!-- routed:XXXX --> `@manual` <!-- xledger-ok:"*` at ~L63/72; back-compat or one-shot migrate); (3) update `tests/test_ledger_seam.sh`; (4) migrate the live annotation on **id:78ff**; (5) audit other HTML-comment-as-meaning uses across the skills ``
  Five list items dragged out of the body and into the head line.

Both items are, unsurprisingly, items ABOUT marker syntax — `5817` is about REVIEW_ME boxes
lacking `<!-- id: -->` markers, `ee62` proposes replacing HTML-comment metadata with visible
annotations. The failure mode selects for exactly the items most likely to discuss it.

**13 lines in `TODO.md` carry a hoisted literal `<!-- id:XXXX -->`** (the non-hex
placeholder), which is the milder half of the same behaviour: a placeholder quoted in prose
is treated as a real marker and hoisted. Cosmetic on its own, but it is the same root
cause and it is the signal that shows how widespread the over-match is.

**What this is NOT, corrected because the first read of it was wrong.** This is not the

severity difference is large: wedged items would be urgent, cosmetic-plus-two-corrupted

**Acceptance**: the keep-set match is anchored to a real marker shape (a bounded token
grammar such as `<!--\s*[a-z-]+:[0-9a-f]{4}\s*-->`, plus whatever bare forms are genuinely
in use) rather than `[^>]*`, so a `<!--` appearing inside backticked prose is never treated
as the start of a marker; the structural catch-all property is PRESERVED for
newly-minted marker TYPES (that is why it exists — do not replace it with a fixed
enumeration of today's four); and `TODO.md:722` and `TODO.md:34` are repaired.
**Done-check**: `grep -c 'id:XXXX' TODO.md` returns 0 for hoisted placeholders, neither
line 722 nor line 34 carries a prose fragment after its `-- detail:` pointer, and
