## From ROADMAP

**The lane grammar is hand-mirrored in three places; make ONE source serve both the actor
and the checker.**

The lane vocabulary is enumerated by hand in at least three places:

* `relay/references/hard-lanes.md` -- the DECLARED single source of truth, which says in its
  own second paragraph that its consumers "MUST agree on its marker set";
* `relay/scripts/classify-repo.sh` -- the `LANE_TAGS` tuple plus the `id:4da4`
  primary-lane anchoring rule (the CHECKER);
* `tools/ledger-shrink.py` -- `_LANE_PATTERNS`, a PRIVATE regex reimplementation (the ACTOR).

**Why an enumeration was the wrong shape here, evidenced rather than asserted.** In one
session the shrinker's lane handling was rebuilt three times off that private copy. Round 1
promoted every body lane token and moved three OPEN items out of human lanes into
pool-carrying ones. Round 2 promoted "when unambiguous" and INVENTED lanes. Round 3
relocated everything and left items lane-less. The rule that finally worked is pure
INVARIANCE against `classify-repo.sh`'s anchoring -- so the shrinker's correctness is
DEFINED by agreement with a grammar it does not read. An enumeration is exactly the thing
that goes stale.

**Both dash spellings stay live.** The fleet is mid-migration from the em dash to the ASCII
spaced hyphen and BOTH spellings are in the corpus, so neither may be normalised to the
other in passing. A parity check that silently canonicalised one spelling would report
agreement that the running consumers do not actually have.

* (b) `classify-repo.sh`'s `LANE_TAGS` recognises every SSOT-declared lane, both dash
  spellings;

yields an empty set passes trivially against every consumer.
