## DENOMINATOR CORRECTION 2026-09-09 -- the cross-repo arm is 0 of 6, not 0 of 92

Caught by the code.lawless session; verified here before correcting.

**The count.** That run dispatched 92 AGENTS but only SIX execute children. From the same
journal:

    grep -o '"label":"execute[^"]*"' <journal> | wc -l   ->  6
    (one unique label, "execute:code.lawless")

    17 write-relay-status   12 release   7 provision   6 integrate   6 execute
     5 mechanical-orphan-draft   5 inject-take   5 heartbeat-beat   ...

`EXECUTE_AGENT_TYPE` binds the **execute lane only** -- review/handoff/hard run Opus and are
out of scope by the flag's own definition -- so the other 86 agents are outside the arm
entirely. Most are mechanical hops and status writes that were never exposed to the death the
flag mitigates.

**Why this is not pedantry: the error inverted its own argument.** The retracted sentence used
a clean 92-agent run as evidence CONSISTENT with a 25% per-child rate. Computed:

    P(0 failures | n=92, p=0.25) = 3.2e-12
    P(0 failures | n=6,  p=0.25) = 0.178

At n=92 a clean run would have been near-PROOF that the rate is nothing like 25% -- that is,
evidence AGAINST this repo's own 7/10 arm. The sentence argued the opposite of what its own
numbers implied, and it did so while defending the item's headline. At the true n=6 the clean
run is unremarkable, which is what the headline actually needs.

**What 0 of 6 does and does not license.** Point estimate 0.00; exact 95% upper bound
`1 - 0.05^(1/6) = 0.393`. So the true rate could be anything up to ~39%. The honest statement
is "0 of 6, consistent with a 25% rate and consistent with much else besides" -- the same
verdict as before at roughly one twelfth of the confidence.

**Label the other two arms by n, not by ratio, for the same reason.** 1 of 4 and 7 of 10 are
small samples too, and the owner's pilot-sample heuristic in the global CLAUDE.md is explicit
that n=10 cannot reliably distinguish rates within ~10pp. A conservative posture toward the
flag is justified by ASYMMETRIC COST (a dead child costs a whole unit and parks an orphan;
passing an extra flag costs nothing), never by mistaking any of these three numbers for a
measured rate. Quote them as `1/4`, `7/10`, `0/6` -- never as `25%`, `70%`, `0%`.

**Untouched by this correction:** the adoption-regression finding above. It rests on no rate
at all -- a mitigation that must be remembered per launch was in fact forgotten, silently,
within five hours, by a session that had it working, and neither session noticed until they
disagreed. That argument gets STRONGER as the success count shrinks, not weaker.
