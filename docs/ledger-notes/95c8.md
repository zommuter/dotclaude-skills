## From TODO

**`control-board.sh`'s "N of them on a human lane" count is wrong for at least one repo, and
the label is what makes it dangerous.** Observed 2026-09-09 while diagnosing why an `--afk`
pool dispatched only one repo.

**The discrepancy.** For `helferli` the board printed:

> Human-lane-only backlog: 19 open ROADMAP item(s), **2 of them on a human lane**
> (`[INPUT -- *]` or `@manual`) and ZERO executable work in any lane

`helferli/ROADMAP.md` has **19** open items and **all 19** carry an `[INPUT ...]` tag
(13 `[INPUT — access]`, 4 `[INPUT — meeting]`, 1 `[INPUT — decision]` em-dash,
1 `[INPUT - decision]` ASCII). The count should be 19, or 19-minus-something-explicable. It
printed 2.

**Three hypotheses TESTED AND REJECTED, recorded so they are not re-run:**

1. **Gating.** Not it, and the numbers run the wrong way: 17 of the 19 are UNGATED, only 2 are
   gated. "2" numerically equals the GATED count, which is a suggestive coincidence but the
   opposite of a human-lane tally.
2. **Delimiter blindness (em dash vs ASCII hyphen).** Not it. `classify-repo.sh`'s `LANE_TAGS`
   / `HUMAN_GATES` list BOTH spellings for every tag (the id:e8d4 two-delimiter alternation),
   and `it-infra` -- whose 30 INPUT items are **26 em-dash + 4 ASCII** -- is counted correctly
   as 30.
3. **Lane vocabulary.** Not it; `[INPUT — meeting]`, `[INPUT — access]` and `[INPUT — decision]`
   are all present in `HUMAN_GATES` in both spellings.

**Control group -- three repos reconcile, one does not.** `it-infra` 30 INPUT → board 30;
`meeting-rpg` 15 → 15; `puzzle-pwa` 19 → 20 (the +1 is plausibly an `@manual`, which the
predicate also counts). Only `helferli` is off, and by 17. A single outlier against three
matches is why this is filed as a defect rather than a misreading of the metric.

**Why it matters despite changing no verdict.** `helferli`'s VERDICT is right either way --
zero executable work, so `human` is correct. The damage is to a human reading the board: "2 of
19 on a human lane" invites the conclusion that 17 items are *something else* (executable,
mis-tagged, or lost), which is exactly the false-starvation reading it sent this session
chasing. A count that disagrees with its own label is worse than no count, because it is acted
on. Note the board is the tracker pilot's pre-registered CONTROL ARM (`id:8066`) -- the
comparator a 4-week fail condition is measured against -- so a miscount here contaminates that
comparison too.

**Acceptance:** a fixture repo whose open items are all `[INPUT ...]` reports every one of them
in the human-lane count; `helferli`'s live count reconciles with
`grep -cE '^- \[ \] *\[INPUT' ROADMAP.md` (plus `@manual`); and the three control repos above
are unchanged. **Done-check:** the new fixture green and `make test` green.

**Deliberately NOT diagnosed further here.** Three wrong theories were floated in one session
before the control group was built; the next step is to read `control-board.sh`'s human-lane
predicate directly against `helferli`'s actual lines, not to advance a fourth guess from the
outside.
