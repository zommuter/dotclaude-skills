## The finding

`relay/scripts/context-budget.sh`'s thresholds are hard-coded for a 200k context window. Every
1M-context child (`claude-opus-5[1m]`) therefore gets a spurious `handback` verdict, because a
transcript that is a small fraction of a 1M window is a large fraction of a 200k one.

## Measured

On the reporting run: `bytes=526474`, `est_tokens=138851` -- roughly 13% of a 1M window, and the
same invocation reports `ok` at `--warn-bytes 1000000 --handback-bytes 1500000`.

## Why it is worse than a bad warning

In zom.fi the ~365 kB baseline transcript alone exceeds the 300000 B default BEFORE any work is
done. Executor-contract rule 2c then fires on the first check, so the unit hands back with zero
commits every single time -- an unconditional livelock, not a threshold that is merely tuned too
tight.

## Related

Same class as the `id:5295` inert-budget-guard window, and the reason executors keep applying the
v16 near-done carve-out by hand (see the 2026-09-09 `id:799f` RELAY_LOG entry, where a `handback`
verdict fired after the fix was already made and the suite already green).

## Shape of a fix, not a decision

The threshold has to become a function of the child's actual window rather than a constant. What
supplies the window (model id, an env var the dispatcher sets, a flag) is the open question and is
not settled here.
