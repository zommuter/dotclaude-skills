## The finding

Running Workflow/pool children ARE addressable mid-run. The address is the child's RAW agent id,
read from `subagents/workflows/<wf>/agent-<id>.meta.json`, passed to `SendMessage`.

Three things that do NOT work as addresses, and one trap:

- The child's LABEL, the workflow RUN id, and the TASK id all fail as addresses.
- `ListAgents` does NOT list such a child until you have sent to it -- the registry is populated
  BY the send. So the child's absence from `ListAgents` is not evidence that it is unreachable.
  That is the trap: the obvious probe reports the opposite of the truth.
