## From TODO

### Why this exists

The ledger LINE GRAMMAR (`id:b048`) is a report-only WARN. On the day it landed, its
author filed **six non-conforming items into `ROADMAP.md`** while actively measuring
non-conformance and dispatching an agent to remove it: `40c0`, `b048`, `7408`, `64f9`,
`ff7c`, `f193`, each with 3 to 5 continuation lines, 9,202 bytes in total. Titles were
fine at 133 to 169 chars; the bodies were exactly the invalid shape. A rule that nothing
enforces gets broken by the person who wrote it, within the hour. That is the argument
for this item, and it is empirical rather than theoretical.

### Shape

A `pre-commit` hook, `hooks/pre-commit-ledger-grammar.sh`, that BLOCKS a commit whose
`git diff --cached` **added lines** introduce a non-conforming line into `TODO.md`,
`ROADMAP.md` or `REVIEW_ME.md`. Unchanged lines are never inspected.

**This is a true ratchet, and the reason matters.** The CLAUDE.md heuristic "a
grandfathering mechanism is not a ratchet" warns that a baseline keyed on an IDENTIFIER
says "this one is forgiven forever" and cannot express a ratchet, because it has no
concept of "modified since baseline" -- the exempted item can regrow without limit. This
hook is keyed on the EDIT, not the item: there is no exemption list, nothing to expire,
and an item can only regrow by adding a line, which is exactly what gets caught.

### Reuse, do not reinvent

`hooks/pre-commit-lane-vocab.sh` (`id:9ef7`) is already this exact shape and is the
template: staged-diff added-lines scan, hard-deny, `--no-verify` as the documented escape
hatch, self-gated to relay-onboarded repos. It also already solves a problem this hook
has -- it masks backticks and takes the leftmost tag by byte position (the `id:4da4`
parser idiom shared with `lane-convert.sh` and `roadmap-lint.sh`), so a prose line that
MENTIONS a lane is not read as carrying one. That is the declarative-versus-referential
distinction, and re-deriving it with a fresh grep is the `id:0d58` false-positive class.

The grammar predicate itself belongs to `relay/scripts/todo-conformance.sh`. Call it;
do not re-implement the grammar in the hook. Two copies of a grammar drift, and the
drift is silent.

### The failure mode that would make it intolerable

`id:7909` is open and records that the lane-vocab ratchet **cannot distinguish a MOVE
from an ADDITION, so it blocks every `roadmap-archive.sh` commit** in a repo carrying
legacy tags. A grammar ratchet is a strictly larger version of that hazard, because
archiving moves whole item blocks rather than single tags.

It probably misses this hook for a structural reason: archiving moves lines INTO
`TODO.archive.md`, and `*.archive.md` is out of grammar scope (`todo-conformance.sh:700`,
`id:2065`), so the added lines land where the rule does not apply. **Probably is not good
enough** -- verify it against a real `archive-done.sh` commit before arming. If it does
bite, this item is gated on `id:7909` and must say so.

### Scope limits, both deliberate

- **Title length stays WARN even in the hook.** 257 existing titles are over budget
  (`id:64f9`), so enforcing titles would block routine edits to any of them and the hook
  would be disabled within a day.
- **Ships DISABLED.** Arming is an explicit `make` target, mirroring
  `make install-lane-ratchet`. This is mechanical sequencing rather than a prose
  instruction not to arm it: `ROADMAP.md` is mid-migration under `id:40c0`, and a hook
  armed against a file that is 96% non-conforming teaches people to pass `--no-verify`,
  which is how a guard dies. Arm it once `40c0` has landed.
