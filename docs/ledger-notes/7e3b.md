## From TODO

Owner-ruled 2026-09-10, filed alongside the orphan-only baseline cleanup.

Both head-line ratchet baselines (`relay/head-length-baseline.txt`, id:0d7c, and
`relay/shape-prose-baseline.txt`, id:2d17) have exactly one regeneration remedy today, and
`--baseline-staleness` prints it: `--regen-length-baseline` / `--regen-shape-baseline` over a
WHOLE ledger. That form cannot express "tighten only". It recaptures every over-budget line
as it stands right now, so a line that GREW since capture, or a line that had no row at all
because it was conforming and has since blown the budget, silently acquires a fresh
grandfathering row at its current size.

Measured on this repo on 2026-09-10: 8 stale rows of 283 in `TODO.md` held 4,493 chars of
slack and there were 5 orphaned rows across both ledgers. Following the printed remedy would
have reclaimed roughly 371 chars of that slack while MINTING 13 new rows forgiving roughly
15,700 chars for items that are over budget today. That is the id-keyed re-grandfathering the
global `CLAUDE.md` heuristic forbids ("a grandfathering mechanism is not a ratchet -- check
what its baseline is KEYED on"): the row's predicate takes an identifier, so it has no concept
of "modified since baseline" and cannot say "may only get better".

The orphan half was taken separately and is DONE: rows forgiving a line that no longer exists
grandfather nothing, so deleting them is strictly a tightening. What remains is the tightening
regen itself.

Wanted contract, row-scoped rather than file-scoped:

- Operates on ONE id's row (or a named set), never a whole-file recapture.
- LOWERS a floor when the current line is shorter than the recorded one.
- REFUSES, loudly and non-zero, to raise a floor or to add a row that does not already exist.
  A line that grew past its floor is a ratchet VIOLATION to be reported, never a row to mint.
- Keeps the existing repo-key + ledger-basename dimensions (id:4839) so a row still cannot
  supply a ceiling for another repo's identically-spelled id.
- The whole-file `--regen-*-baseline` path stays for the genuine first capture of a NEW
  ledger; the staleness report should stop printing it as the remedy for stale rows.
