### The defect: a global keep-token strip can HIDE a long title

`id:60eb` made the title measurement strip chrome, correctly -- a mandatory `-- detail:`
pointer must not push an item over budget. But `strip_chrome` applies `LENGTH_MUST_KEEP_RE`
**globally** (`//g`) to the title. The old step 5 stripped only LEADING bracket groups, so a
keep-token sitting in mid-PROSE was still counted as title. It no longer is.

Four hand-built fixtures, each ~201 chars of pure prose, all flagged by the old script and
**silent under the new one**:

| fixture prose contains | chars | old | new |
|---|---|---|---|
| `BLOCKED on` | 201 | `title-long (201)` | **no finding** |
| `[HARD]` | 202 | `title-long (202)` | **no finding** |
| the gate glyph | 201 | `title-long (201)` | **no finding** |
| `gated-on:abcd` | 201 | `title-long (201)` | **no finding** |

`BLOCKED on` alone steals 10 chars; two tokens steal 20. **This is the direction that hides
violations**, which is why it is filed even though severity is low: the band is narrow
(~200-215 for one token) and the class is WARN-only.

### It is LATENT, not active -- measured

**Zero live false negatives on either ledger today.** Both live `BLOCKED on` titles (`TODO.md`
HEAD L354, L682) are legitimate strips: the token sits in the shrinker-parked trailing run,
not in prose.

Separately, and less severe: **59 live TODO titles and 5 ROADMAP titles lose characters from
inside the real title region** -- e.g. a title whose SUBJECT is a lane name has that lane name
deleted before measurement. None flips a verdict today. Two examples worth reading because
they are self-referential:

- ``Resolving an `[INPUT - decision]` item leaves its lane tag stale`` -- the backticked lane
  name IS the subject and is deleted.
- `id:5ed7`, whose entire content is that unanchored substring matching re-applies a marker to
  anything that merely WRITES about it. The title measurement now reproduces the bug that item
  describes.

### The narrowing, not decided here

Anchor the keep-token strip to the region at/after the pointer, or to a TRAILING run, rather
than globally. That keeps `id:60eb`'s fix (chrome after the title does not count) while
restoring the property that prose in the title counts even when it quotes a token.

Reviewer's judgement, which I share: not their call to make mid-review, and not worth
reverting `60eb` over given zero live instances.

### Test gap that lets it recur silently

`tests/test_todo_conformance_title_chrome_60eb.sh` case (c) is the only guard against
UNDER-reporting, and neither of its fixtures (`aa03`, `aa07`) contains a keep-token inside
genuinely-long prose -- so it structurally cannot catch this class.

**The fixture belongs in THIS item's own RED spec, not in `60eb`'s test. I tried the other
way first and it was wrong twice over:**

1. **It was VACUOUS as built.** `${LONG:0:150} BLOCKED on ${LONG:150:60}` measures 222 chars
   and 211 after the strip -- over budget BOTH ways, so it is flagged either way and proves
   nothing. The false-negative band is NARROW: roughly 201-210 for a single 10-char token,
   because the token must be what carries the title across 200. A fixture for this class has
   to be built to the band, and a fixture built by eye will miss it.
2. **It belongs in a RED spec.** `a580` is an OPEN defect, so a fixture that pins the CORRECT
   behaviour must FAIL today. Putting it in `60eb`'s test would make that file red and block
   ticking an item whose own fix is complete and independently reviewed. `60eb` is done; this
   is not; they need separate files.

So the acceptance below wants a NEW test carrying `# roadmap:a580`, expected-red until this
lands, with its fixture sized to the band and that sizing asserted (the way
`tests/test_shape_regrowth_below_baseline_cf64.sh` asserts its own trap is armed before
relying on it).
