## 5. Gotchas learned this run

- **Install drift nearly broke the whole run.** `lib-publish-remote.sh` and `relay-log-archive.sh`
  were committed but never installed. Step 0c is warn-only by design, but this was a TRUE positive
  with a concrete consequence — `integrate.sh` step 6d handbacks `EX_WIRING` if the archiver is
  missing, i.e. **every integrate would have failed**. Ran `make install-relay` before launching.
  **Always run 0c and read it, even though it never refuses.**
- **The front-door EXIT teardown trap (step 3b) cannot be honoured from this harness** — each Bash
  call is a fresh shell, so a `trap` does not persist. Teardown was run by hand after the Workflow
  returned. If a session dies mid-run, the heartbeat + leases wait for their mtime TTL.
- **`--exclude` does not sandbox scripts.** Per-file symlinks mean this repo's `integrate.sh`,
  budgets and allowlist were live for every repo the pool touched. `--exclude dotclaude-skills`
  only kept it out of the work queue.
- **The gate's Sonnet budget is nearly irrelevant for sliced units.** A sliced execute unit sizes
  at ~66.5k against 100,000 and dispatches regardless of ledger size; what actually bounds it is
  the ~176k ceiling reached DURING work, which no dispatch-time gate can see.
