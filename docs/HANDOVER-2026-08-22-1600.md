## Traps this session paid for

- `git-lock-push.sh` with no `--remote` pushes to **ALL** remotes. That is the default, documented in its own header. Never omit it in a skill that runs unattended.
- `stop-request.sh` writes a **targeted** sentinel and fires at the **next round boundary** — it does NOT kill in-flight children. If the goal is to stop spending immediately, that is `--now` (`TaskStop`), at the cost of orphaned worktrees.

- `md-merge.py` `insert_after` needs an anchor id that **already exists** — chaining new item N+1 onto new item N in one payload always fails. Anchor every insert on an existing id.
